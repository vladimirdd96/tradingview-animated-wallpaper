## Design Context

### Users

TradingView Desktop users choosing and controlling animated backgrounds while working in their existing chart session. The creator explicitly wants settings discoverable in the top-right three-dot menu and a seamless native appearance.

### Brand Personality

Native, quiet, precise. Match TradingView's own controls and visual language; remove separate companion branding and promotional copy from the settings window.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the actual installed TradingView menu and settings as the reference. Follow its selected light/dark/system appearance, system typography, neutral surfaces, compact form rows, blue focus/accent, and ordinary menu grouping. Do not introduce a new font family, decorative palette, or marketing layout.

### Design Principles

1. Place Wallpaper settings on its own full-width menu row next to Settings.
2. Keep familiar controls, comfortable targets, keyboard access, and visible focus.
3. Synchronize appearance with TradingView while the window is open.
4. Preserve wallpaper preferences, chart interaction, and the running app session.
5. Make settings concise and immediately applied, with a clear Done action.
