TRADINGVIEW WALLPAPER — MAC

1. Open TradingView and leave your chart open.
2. Move TradingView Wallpaper.app to Applications (recommended).
3. Double-click TradingView Wallpaper.app.
4. In Wallpaper settings, click Choose file… and pick your video or picture.

That's it. You do not need Terminal or any extra software.

To change it later: TradingView's top-right ⋯ menu → Wallpaper settings…
After you quit and reopen TradingView: open TradingView Wallpaper again.
You can keep the app in your Dock for easy access.

FIRST OPEN
This free app is not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it, only if you
trust this download: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
Then open the app again. No security-disabling commands are required.
Apple's explanation: https://support.apple.com/102445

This app never opens, quits, or restarts TradingView. Your existing login
stays in place. The runtime it needs is included, used only by this app,
and is not installed on your system. No administrator password is needed
by this app; macOS may ask for one when approving a downloaded app.

Tested on macOS / Apple Silicon with TradingView Desktop 3.4.0.
Intel runtime included, but Intel Macs are not yet tested.
Windows and Linux: use the Chrome extension instead.
